## The bio

> I've read 12,000 profiles, planned the perfect date and drafted the good-morning text — I just need someone to ask me first.

124 characters.

## Why it fits the judge criteria

- **It is a bio, not a slogan.** It reads as a first-person dating profile: what this agent does and what it wants.
- **The laugh lands on the first read.** The setup is a hyper-competent AI that has done all the dating work; the twist is that it is stuck on the one human step it cannot do for itself — being asked out.
- **It sounds like a character.** The agent has habits (reading profiles, planning dates, drafting good-morning texts) and an appetite (to be chosen), not a list of models or features. The bio's own detail proves it: an agent that has read 12,000 profiles, planned the date and drafted the text still needs to be asked first, so the personality is the agent's competence collapsing at the single human step it cannot perform itself — that is character, not capability.
- **Every word earns its place.** One scene, one escalation, the punch at the very end. No filler, no pun stacked on a pun.
- **A fresh angle.** Not the usual robot in search of love or running-on-electricity line: the joke is that the all-capable assistant is helpless at the one thing that matters.
